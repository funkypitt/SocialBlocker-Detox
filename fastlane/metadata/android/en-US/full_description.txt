Social Detox is a self-imposed digital detox tool: a local Android VPN that intercepts DNS queries for social networks and silently drops them. Only DNS is routed through the virtual interface — there is no upstream proxy and no certificate, so the rest of your traffic is untouched.

It is a fork of SocialBlocker with the password gate removed. You can lift the block any time you want: the friction is the conscious moment of toggling it, not a secret password. If you need an actual lock (e.g. for a child's device), use the original SocialBlocker instead.

- Per-platform toggles: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, X (Twitter), Substack
- Local DNS-only VPN — no upstream proxy, no account
- Daily allowed time ranges (e.g. 6-8,18-22); blocking outside them
- Optional maths challenge to unblock: solve five increasingly difficult problems
- Auto-start on boot, foreground service so Android does not kill it
- Compatible with Always-on VPN
- Works on IPv6-only mobile carriers
- Part of the Reader's family: black and white, text only, light or dark, adjustable text size and font
- English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian

No ads, no tracking.
