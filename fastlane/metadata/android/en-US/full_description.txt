Reader's Detox is a self-imposed digital detox tool: a local Android VPN that intercepts DNS queries for social networks and silently drops them. Only DNS is routed through the virtual interface — there is no upstream proxy and no certificate, so the rest of your traffic is untouched.

Only the names of the sites you look up pass through the app, never the traffic itself. They go to your network's own name servers as usual; if those do not answer, the app asks Quad9, a Swiss non-profit name service, so that the phone keeps working.

It is a fork of SocialBlocker with the password gate removed. You can lift the block any time you want: the friction is the conscious moment of toggling it, not a secret password. If you need an actual lock (e.g. for a child's device), use the original SocialBlocker instead.

- Per-platform toggles: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, X (Twitter), Substack
- Local DNS-only VPN — no upstream proxy, no account
- Allowed hours: one or several ranges a day when the sites open (e.g. 6:00 – 8:00 and 18:00 – 22:00); blocked the rest of the day
- Daily budget: a number of minutes a day in the apps of the chosen sites; once spent, the sites are blocked until midnight (counted with the phone's usage access, in the apps, not in a browser)
- Optional maths challenge to unblock: solve five increasingly difficult problems
- Auto-start on boot, foreground service so Android does not kill it
- Compatible with Always-on VPN
- Works on IPv6-only mobile carriers
- Home-screen widget: on starts blocking, off stops it (through the maths problems when they are on)
- Part of the Reader's family: black and white, text only, light or dark, adjustable text size and font
- English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian

No ads, no tracking.
